What to do

Make Dice immutable, repair everything that breaks, and then build a Card of your own that answers the same question about itself.

  • Change all three Dice properties to { get; init; } and fix both call sites the compiler flags, leaving set where it lies.
  • Confirm ReferenceEquals(first, second) still prints True, and be able to say why that is the point of the whole lesson.
  • Create your own Card.cs and build a Card that makes a deliberate call about what is fixed and what can change.
  • Be ready to name one person who would have designed it differently and say what they would have wanted instead.  "This is just correct" leaves the question unanswered.

Where am I?

Standard
Declare immutability member by member on a record, repair the call sites that depended on mutation, and explain what that bought.
