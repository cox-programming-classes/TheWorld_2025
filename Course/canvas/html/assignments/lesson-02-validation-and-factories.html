What to do

Give Dice the two rules it is missing, grow TryParse until the rulebook loads correctly, and then make your own Card impossible to build wrong.

  • Add guards for sides and modifier inside Of(), and be able to say why that is the place for them.
  • Grow TryParse until Scene 2 loads 2d6 d20 3d8+2 1d4-1 4d6 2d6 1d20+3 and refuses everything else.
  • Add three factory archetypes that call Of() and leave the rules where they are.
  • In your own Card.cs: break it on purpose first, then make the constructor private and put every rule behind a factory.
  • Be ready to answer:  which of your rules protect against a bug in your code, and which against bad data from outside it?

Where am I?

Standard
Make a type impossible to construct in an invalid state, using a private constructor and composing factory methods, and choose correctly between throwing and the Try pattern.
Getting StartedGuards still to write, or the build is red.  Next step: look at how the count guard inside Of() is written -- check the value, throw with a message naming the parameter, then get out.  Copy that shape for sides.  If you are getting CS9135, you tried to use MaxSides in a pattern; a pattern requires a const and this is a property, so write it as sides < MinSides || sides > MaxSides instead.

Went Beyond…and I went after something the lesson left open -- an enum-based rank that makes "11H" impossible to express, a Result type in place of exceptions, a factory that composes so cleanly a new archetype is one line, or a real argument about where "2 d 6" belongs that I can defend from both sides.
